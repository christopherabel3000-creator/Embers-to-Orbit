## Phase 3 (Nation era)

The Nation era was planned with Christopher on October 14, 2026 (DESIGN.md, The Nation era), and is built in five phases.

### Done

- Phase 1, the nation at home: more settlements (from the capital or a large settlement), each with a role (farming, timber, mining, market town, or fortress), demands, and defense and learning priorities; named governors with skills who grow, age, and are succeeded; loyalty with its reasons, aid, and defiance; settlements counted by groups, with buildings their governors raise; fortresses that meet raiders on the road; roads; the Learning branch (Writing, Mathematics, Astronomy, Schools); a capital that counts its people by group past 100; the Nation and Settlement screens.

- Phase 2, the wider world: seven regions around the valley with 25 hand-crafted places; expeditions (send and wait) with finds, dangers, and choices on the road; small peoples, wonders, and ruins, and a Far horizons tradition; iron and salt from mining towns, iron tools and salting; settlements beyond the valley; River boats, Mapmaking, Mining, Iron working, and Salting; the World and Region screens.

- Phase 3, the first great nations: the Ostrani of the Ostran delta and the Orhai of the far steppe, with homelands, rumors, and first contact; envoys, trade in lots for coin, embassies, trade and peace pacts, marriages, and trade routes; Orhai raids on settlements within their reach and demands for tribute; an Ostrani merchant fleet; coin from trade, routes, taxes, and silver, governors' wages, and hiring for coin; laws on inheritance, worship, and taxes; the Velari and Tellani joining the nation, and the Skarn as vassals; Coinage, Weights and measures, Fine crafts, Trade routes, Guild charters or Free markets, Law codes, Taxes, and Diplomacy.

- Phase 4, armies: armies of up to eight companies of six kinds (spearmen, shield infantry, archers, cavalry, skirmishers, and siege engines) raised from the capital and the settlements, under named commanders with traits and growing skill; marches across the regions slowed by winter and mud and sped by roads; food carried and supply from the stores; orders to hold, march, raid, besiege, or hold the high pass, and stances for when a battle comes; field battles on the ground of each region; armies at home that meet raiders on the road, harry and attack besiegers, relieve allies, and bring defiant settlements to heel; wars declared with or without a reason, war weariness, enemy hosts against settlements, raids and sieges the player lays, storming, and peace on terms (even, tribute, land, vassals, or bought); horses and carts; Iron weapons, Horse breeding, Horse riding, Cavalry, Siege engines, Logistics, Signal beacons, Fortresses (the stone keep), and Levies or a Standing army; the Armies, Army, and Field battle screens.

- Phase 5, toward the sea: the other five great nations (the Keshari, the Dravan, the Ashani, the Haldi, and the Venn) in five new homelands, with their own trade, signatures, and, for the Dravan and the Haldi, raids; ships along the coast, the outer isles reached by sea, ports and harbors; Crop rotation, the plough, Mills, Fishing boats, Bridges, Wells and cisterns, Shipbuilding, Harbors, Navigation, Temples, Medicine, and a crown or an assembly; and the voyage across the open sea that ends the era (a capital of 100, four settlements, Navigation and a harbor, three great nations met). A fix for a capital that ran out of baby names, which froze the game.

### Done after the five phases

- Settlers for coin (October 16, 2026): with Coinage, coin pays the passage of settlers to the capital, a party of up to 5 each season, at a price that rises with the capital; a steward's standing order does it each season, keeping a reserve. Simulated nations send the voyage in about year 38 or 39 instead of 42, and finish with about 280 coin instead of 3,200.
- Moving out of the capital (October 16, 2026): people move from the capital into the nation's settlements, up to 10 a season to each, as far as their homes have room; a steward's standing order keeps the capital at a chosen size. With the capital kept near 120, simulated nations end at year 45 with a capital of 120 instead of 210 and settlements of about 55 people instead of 34, and the voyage comes as before.
